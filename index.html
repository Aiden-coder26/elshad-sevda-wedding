How We Met

Our Love Story

"Some meetings feel less like a first encounter and more like a long-awaited homecoming."

It all began on a crisp autumn evening in Baku over shared cups of tea and endless conversations about art, travel, and dreams. What started as a chance meeting quickly blossomed into an inseparable partnership filled with adventure and laughter.

After five years of exploring the world together, building a home, and supporting each other's aspirations, Elshad proposed during a sunset by the Caspian Sea. Now, we are overjoyed to begin this next beautiful chapter surrounded by our favorite people.
